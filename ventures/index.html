I’m interested in businesses built around useful products and services. My starting point is the problem, the people it affects and the work needed to solve it.

An engineering approach

How I evaluate an idea.

01 / The need

Who is it for?

Understand the people, the problem and the alternatives they already have.

02 / The value

What makes it useful?

Define what the idea improves and why someone would choose it.

03 / The work

What will it take?

Think through the resources, constraints and decisions involved in building it.
